From image to particle count in three steps

Your analyses are saved to your account, so you can compare samples and export results later.

  1. Upload an image or video

    Drop in a photo of a filter, slide or water sample, or an MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV or WEBM clip.

  2. Set how strict to be

    Raise the confidence threshold to keep only certain detections, or lower it to catch faint particles.

  3. Review and export

    Check the marked image or video, read the particle data, then download the result or a CSV.

Reading the results

Detections are a screening aid. Confirm important findings with spectroscopy such as FTIR or Raman.

  • ConfidenceHow sure the model is that a box holds a microplastic. Each box shows its score.
  • Particle sizeSizes are in pixels. Enter your microns-per-pixel value to get micrometres.
  • Video countsWith tracking on, each particle keeps one ID across frames, so the unique count isn't inflated by repeat sightings. A particle that leaves and re-enters view can be counted twice.
  • CoverageShare of the image covered by detection boxes. Overlapping boxes can overstate it.
